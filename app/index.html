    Roadmap to the platinum

      Missable trophies — do these before they pass

      Story missions in GTA games can be replayed, so chapter and mission trophies are not missable. The seven below are flagged missable because their official descriptions describe a one-off moment, a choice, or a run that must be done in one go — miss the moment and it may not come back. Each flag says why, from the description itself.

        Spoiler setting

        With Hide story spoilers on, story trophies you have not earned yet show as “Hidden story trophy” — their names and descriptions stay hidden until you tick them earned. Collectibles, activities and the platinum are never hidden. The setting is saved in this browser.

        About the sources

        Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list as Exophase and PSNProfiles listed it (the two agree on every name, description and grade). PSN counts — such as 42 Shooting Range challenges — come from PSNProfiles. Every guide is written in our own words from the official description; where a place or step is not pinned down by a source we have checked, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing one.

        No online trophies: the official list has no online trophy, so the Online filter is empty by design. This guide is unofficial, works fully offline, and uses no Rockstar logo or official artwork.
